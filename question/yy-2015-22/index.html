Part A
Directions:
Read the following four texts. Answer the questions below each text by choosing A, B,
C or D. Mark your answers on the ANSWER SHEET. (40 points)

A new study suggests that contrary to most surveys, people are actually more stressed at home than at work. Researchers measured people’s cortisol, which is a stress marker, while they were at work and while they were at home and found it higher at what is supposed to be a place of refuge.

“Further contradicting conventional wisdom, we found that women as well as men have lower levels of stress at work than at home,” writes one of the researchers, Sarah Damaske. In fact women even say they feel better at work, she notes. “It is men, not women, who report being happier at home than at work.” Another surprise is that the findings hold true for both those with children and without, but more so for nonparents. This is why people who work outside the home have better health.

What the study doesn’t measure is whether people are still doing work when they’re at home, whether it is household work or work brought home from the office. For many men, the end of the workday is a time to kick back. For women who stay home, they never get to leave the office. And for women who work outside the home, they often are playing catch-up-with-household tasks. With the blurring of roles, and the fact that the home front lags well behind the workplace in making adjustments for working women, it’s not surprising that women are more stressed at home.

But it’s not just a gender thing. At work, people pretty much know what they’re supposed to be doing: working, making money, doing the tasks they have to do in order to draw an income. The bargain is very pure: Employee puts in hours of physical or mental labor and employee draws out life-sustaining moola.

On the home front, however, people have no such clarity. Rare is the household in which the division of labor is so clinically and methodically laid out. There are a lot of tasks to be done, there are inadequate rewards for most of them. Your home colleagues – your family – have no clear rewards for their labor; they need to be talked into it, or if they’re teenagers, threatened with complete removal of all electronic devices. Plus, they’re your family. You cannot fire your family. You never really get to go home from home.

So it’s not surprising that people are more stressed at home. Not only are the tasks apparently infinite, the co-workers are much harder to motivate.

According to Damaske, who are likely to be the happiest at home?

答案

答案：B

22

本题讲解：2015 阅读第 22 题 · 在家最快乐的人＝性别线 × 子女线 交叉出的交集

答案：B（Childless husbands 无子女的丈夫）。

第 0 步：先立机制——happiest 是两个维度的"双最大值"

题干问 According to Damaske，谁在家最快乐。定位段回答"在家幸福感"用了两个维度：一是性别维度——"声称在家比上班更快乐的是男性而非女性"（It is men, not women, who report being happier at home than at work）；二是子女维度——"该发现对有无子女者都适用，但对无子女者更甚"（hold true for both those with children and without, but more so for nonparents）。"在家最快乐"必须同时占据两个维度的最高点：男性 × 无子女。单看任何一句都只能得一半，答案藏在两句的交集里。

第 1 步：定位原文

"It is men, not women, who report being happier at home than at work." Another surprise is that the findings hold true for both those with children and without, but more so for nonparents.
  • ③句（性别线）：男性（不是女性）在家更快乐；
  • ④句（子女线）：无子女者（more so for nonparents）这种"在家更快乐"更明显。

两条线相乘：无子女的男性 ＝ childless husbands。

第 2 步：对号入座

选项词义判定理由
A Working mothers有工作的母亲✗ 排除女性：性别线已出局；working 维度原文也未比较——双重不符
B Childless husbands无子女的丈夫✓ 当选男性（③句）× 无子女（④句 more so for nonparents）＝ 在家幸福感双最大值
C Working fathers有工作的父亲✗ 排除性别线达标（男性），但子女线只拿到"有子女"档，而④句明确"无子女者更甚"
D Childless wives无子女的妻子✗ 排除子女线达标，但性别线反了——③句强调 It is men, not women

第 3 步：预判追问

  • 为什么不选含 working 的 A/C？第二段明明谈到了上班？：该段比较的是人们在家与上班时的感受差，并没有把"职业人士 vs 专职在家人士"作为一组对比去比"谁在家更快乐"。working 是选项造出来的干扰维度，原文没有这个比较。
  • more so for nonparents 修饰的是什么？：修饰"在家比上班快乐"这一发现的适用程度——对有无子女者都成立，但无子女者更突出。所以子女维度上，无子女者赢。

易错点

  • 误选 C（Working fathers）：只读了③句的性别线，漏掉④句的子女线，没做交集。
  • 误选 D（Childless wives）：只读了④句的子女线，被 not women 的强调句式漏读。
  • 误选 A（Working mothers）：性别、子女两条线全反；还叠加 working 干扰维度。
记忆锚点："谁最 X"题先拆维度（性别 × 子女），找两个最大值的交集；It is men, not women 强调句式锁定性别；more so for nonparents 锁定子女线；working 是原文没有的比较维度。
