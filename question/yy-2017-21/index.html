Read the following four texts. Answer the questions after each text by choosing A, B, C or D. Mark your answers on the ANSWER SHEET. (40 points)

Every Saturday morning, at 9 am, more than 50,000 runners set off to run 5km around their local park. The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad. Events are free, staffed by thousands of volunteers. Runners range from four years old to grandparents; their times range from Andrew Baddeley’s world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour.

Parkrun is succeeding where London’s Olympic “legacy” is failing. Ten years ago on Monday, it was announced that the Games of the 30th Olympiad would be in London. Planning documents pledged that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches. The population would be fitter, healthier and produce more winners. It has not happened. The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise, by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012 – but the general population was growing faster. Worse, the numbers are now falling at an accelerating rate. The opposition claims primary school pupils doing at least two hours of sport a week have nearly halved. Obesity has risen among adults and children. Official retrospections continue as to why London 2012 failed to “inspire a generation.” The success of Parkrun offers answers.

Parkun is not a race but a time trial: Your only competitor is the clock. The ethos welcomes anybody. There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line as there is about top talent shining. The Olympic bidders, by contrast, wanted to get more people doing sport and to produce more elite athletes. The dual aim was mixed up: The stress on success over taking part was intimidating for newcomers.

Indeed, there is something a little absurd in the state getting involved in the planning of such a fundamentally “grassroots” concept as community sports associations. If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods – making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools. But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education. Instead of wordy, worthy strategies, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive. Or at least not make them worse.

According to Paragraph 1, Parkrun has .

答案：A

本题讲解：2017 Text 1 第 21 题 · 第一段四句细节的共同因子

答案：A（gained great popularity）。

第 0 步：先立机制——"According to Paragraph 1" 是概括题的信号

题干问 According to Paragraph 1, Parkrun has ____，四个选项全是抽象概括（赢得了欢迎 / 创造了岗位 / 加强了社区联系 / 成了官方节日），而原文给的是一串具体细节。这类题的机制：细节是表、概括是底——把每句细节的"公共因子"提炼出来，再看哪个选项能把全部细节盖住。盖不住的选项，就是被篡改、臆断出来的干扰项。

第 1 步：定位原文（首段四句）

① Every Saturday morning, at 9 am, more than 50,000 runners set off to run 5km around their local park. ② The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad. ③ Events are free, staffed by thousands of volunteers. ④ Runners range from four years old to grandparents...

第 2 步：提炼公共因子

句细节提炼
①每周六 9 点开跑、超过 5 万人规模巨大、已成惯例
②十几个朋友起步 → 英国内外 400+ 场发展迅猛
③免费举办、数千志愿者撑起吸引力强、有人气
④4 岁小孩到爷爷奶奶都在跑覆盖老少全人群

四句的公共因子只有一条：参与者多、覆盖广、发展快 = 大受欢迎。

第 3 步：逐项排除

选项判定理由与典型误解
A gained great popularity✓ 当选对四句细节的高度概括；下段首句 Parkrun is succeeding 反向验证成立。
B created many jobs✗ 排除把③句 staffed by thousands of volunteers（由志愿者义务撑起）歪成"创造岗位"：志愿者无薪，恰说明不是岗位。（典型误解：见"很多人参与活动"就联想到"就业"。）
C strengthened community ties✗ 排除文中只说"多人参加"，没有任何人际互动的描写；local park、a dozen friends 只是背景。（典型误解：把"社区里的活动"脑补成"社区关系紧密"。）
D become an official festival✗ 排除方向反了：events are free + 志愿者撑起，恰是民间自发活动；world record 说明有人跑得快，不说明官方性。（典型误解：由"世界纪录"推出"官方赛事"。）

第 4 步：预判追问

  • 怎么验证概括是否成立？：用下段首句反推——Parkrun is succeeding where London's Olympic "legacy" is failing。作者明说公园跑在"成功"，与"大受欢迎"互为印证。
  • 为什么不是细节题？：四个选项在字面上都不等于原文任何一句——若逐词找"同义替换"会扑空；凡选项全是上位概括、原文全是下位细节，就是主旨型细节题。

易错点

  • 误选 B（created many jobs）：volunteer（志愿者）与 job（有酬岗位）含义相反，被"人数多"带偏。
  • 误选 C（strengthened community ties）：把"地点在社区"偷换成"关系在社区"。
记忆锚点：Paragraph 1 + 概括型选项 = 细节求公共因子；志愿者 ≠ 岗位；下段首句 is succeeding 是现成的验证器。
