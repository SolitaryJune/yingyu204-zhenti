yy-2020-48

15 分

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.
You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

范文

The figures in the pie chart are based on a survey on the purpose of mobile reading among students at a certain university. Over three quarters of respondents read on their cellphones for the sake of acquiring knowledge (59.5%) or obtaining information (17.0%), while roughly one fifth do so just for fun.

The chart indicates that mobile reading, once regarded merely as a way to kill time, has become an approach to improving oneself or expanding one's horizons. It is actually a predictable phenomenon, given the popularization of smartphones. They provide us with easy online access, thereby catering to our need for a variety of information. The increasingly intense competition in the job market has also played a part. In order to gain nothing more than a slight edge over other job applicants, we college students have to keep learning something new, mastering practical skills and receiving real-time information.

In conclusion, mobile reading is bound to rise in popularity. Richer content and a better reading experience will be the key to attracting users.

自评得分：
48

本题讲解：饼状图作文——"功能对比"描图 + 客观主观双因析因（2020 大作文）

答案（参考范文口径）：The figures in the pie chart are based on a survey on the purpose of mobile reading among students at a certain university. Over three quarters of respondents read on their cellphones for the sake of acquiring knowledge (59.5%) or obtaining information (17.0%), while roughly one fifth do so just for fun. The chart indicates that mobile reading, once regarded merely as a way to kill time, has become an approach to improving oneself or expanding one's horizons. It is actually a predictable phenomenon, given the popularization of smartphones. They provide us with easy online access, thereby catering to our need for a variety of information. The increasingly intense competition in the job market has also played a part. In order to gain nothing more than a slight edge over other job applicants, we college students have to keep learning something new, mastering practical skills and receiving real-time information. In conclusion, mobile reading is bound to rise in popularity. Richer content and a better reading experience will be the key to attracting users.

第 0 步：先立机制——两问换三段，饼图的重心在"主要对比"

指令两问：interpret the chart（描图）+ give your comments（评论）。评论不是空发感想，而是"点破现象 → 析因 → 顺因预测"，所以全文落成三段：描图段 → 析因段 → 预测段。饼图是比例分布图，四类数据不能平均用力地罗列——描图的任务是提炼出一组主要对比，让第二段有的放矢。机制立住了，每一段都是在往这组对比上长内容。

第 1 步：读图——先看标题定对象，再排数据找对比

  • 标题"某高校学生手机阅读目的调查"给出调查对象（某高校学生）与调查内容（手机阅读的目的）——概述句的两个填空直接来自标题。
  • 四类数据从高到低：学习知识 59.5% > 消磨时间 21.3% > 获取信息 17.0% > 其他 2.2%。
  • 关键运算：学习知识 + 获取信息 = 76.5%，超过四分之三，而"消磨时间"只占约五分之一。这组对比就是全文的"矛盾"：手机阅读的主流用途是求知与获取信息，娱乐只是次要功能。第二段的"现象"和第三段的"预测"都从这组长出来。

第 2 步：描图段——概述句 + 核心数据句

  • 概述句交代数据来源：The figures in the pie chart are based on a survey on the purpose of mobile reading among students at a certain university.（基于一项针对某高校学生手机阅读目的的调查）
  • 核心数据句一句装下主要对比：Over three quarters of respondents read on their cellphones for the sake of acquiring knowledge (59.5%) or obtaining information (17.0%), while roughly one fifth do so just for fun.
  • 分数替换：over three quarters（超四分之三）替代 76.5%，roughly one fifth（约五分之一）替代 21.3%——分数比连续四个百分数更有美感，关键百分比括注保留精确性；
  • while 一词完成两类用途的对比；do so 回指 read on their cellphones，避免重复；
  • respondent = 受访者、调查对象，调查图表作文的标配词。
  • 只报 59.5% 一项、或把四个百分比从头念到尾，都是描图段不合格的写法：前者数据不完整，后者没有重心。

第 3 步：析因段——先点破现象，再一客观一主观双因展开

  • 现象句（承描图段）：The chart indicates that mobile reading, once regarded merely as a way to kill time, has become an approach to improving oneself or expanding one's horizons.——插入语 once regarded...（曾经仅被视为消遣）制造"今昔反差"；现在完成时 has become 点明转变已经发生；improving oneself / expanding one's horizons（提升自我、拓宽眼界）回应"学习知识 + 获取信息"两大用途。
  • 原因一（客观）：智能手机的普及。It is actually a predictable phenomenon, given the popularization of smartphones.（given 复合介词 = 考虑到）→ They provide us with easy online access, thereby catering to our need for a variety of information.（thereby + 现在分词作结果；cater to = 满足需求）——设备随处可联网，满足各类信息需求。
  • 原因二（主观）：就业竞争倒逼自我提升。The increasingly intense competition in the job market has also played a part.（play a part 引出补充原因，also 标志第二条）→ In order to gain nothing more than a slight edge over other job applicants（哪怕只比求职者略胜一筹）, we college students have to keep learning something new, mastering practical skills and receiving real-time information（三个动名词并列：学新知、练技能、接资讯）。
  • 两条原因一外一内，共同指向"手机阅读功能升级"——析因不是凑句子，是同一现象的两个侧面。

第 4 步：预测段——顺因推势，不凭空展望

In conclusion 收束：手机阅读必然继续流行（is bound to rise in popularity）——这个"必然"不是拍脑袋，它顺延析因段的两个原因：智能机继续普及（客观）+ 竞争持续存在（主观）。第二句点明发展的关键因素：更丰富的内容与更优质的阅读体验将决定谁能吸引用户（Richer content and a better reading experience will be the key to attracting users.）。一句趋势 + 一句关键，预测段就立住了。

第 5 步：语言纪律——预判"怎么不丢冤枉分"

  • 时态纪律：饼图是调查的常态结果，描图用一般现在时（read / are based on）；现象已发生用现在完成时（has become / has played a part）；预测用 will / be bound to。一个时态走到底是典型扣分点。
  • 搭配红线：gain / acquire knowledge（learn knowledge 是错误搭配）；obtain information；kill time（消磨时间，比 play 有分量）。
  • 衔接词分工：while（描图对比）、given（让步原因）、also（补第二条原因）、thereby（结果）、In conclusion（总结）。
  • 百分数表达：分数（over three quarters / roughly one fifth）与括注百分比配合，避免通篇 59.5%、21.3% 的机械堆砌。

易错点：每个失分点对应哪种误解

  • 罗列四个百分比不提炼：把"其他 2.2%"当重点逐项报数——描图没有重心，第二段无处生长；正确是抓住"求知 + 信息 ≈ 76.5% vs 消遣 21.3%"这组主对比。
  • 只写 59.5% 一项：数据不完整，compare 的"另一侧"（消遣）丢失，对比无从谈起。
  • learn knowledge：高频搭配错误；知识用 gain / acquire / get，information 才与 obtain / receive 搭。
  • 析因写成"手机危害论"：写沉迷、视力下降——偏离图表传达的积极趋势（手机阅读是提升自我的途径）；析因必须解释数据反映的现象。
  • 预测凭空抒情：I believe mobile reading will have a bright future 一句空话；要从原因顺延（普及持续 + 竞争持续 → 必然更流行），并落到一个具体关键（内容与体验）。
  • 图表类型写错：pie chart 写成 chart/graph 混用尚可，写成 diagram/table 则类型失实；描图第一句就把"饼图 + 调查 + 对象"交代清。
记忆锚点：饼图描图 = 概述句（来源 + 对象）+ 主对比句；分数替换 + while + do so；析因 = 现象句（once...has become）+ 一客观一主观；预测 = be bound to + 关键因素；搭配红线 gain knowledge / obtain information。
