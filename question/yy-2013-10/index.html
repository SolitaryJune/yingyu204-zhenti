Read the following text. Choose the best word( s) for each numbered blank and mark A, B, C or D on the ANSWER SHEET.

Given the advantages of electronic money, you might think that we would move quickly to the cashless society in which all payments are made electronically. __1__, a true cashless society is probably not around the corner. Indeed, predictions have been __2__ for two decades but have not yet come to fruition. For example, Business Week predicted in 1975 that electronic means of payment would soon “revolutionize the very __3__ of money itself,” only to __4__ itself several years later. Why has the movement to a cashless society been so __5__ in coming?

Although electronic means of payment may be more efficient than a payments system based on paper, several factors work __6__ the disappearance of the paper system. First, it is very __7__ to set up the computer, card reader, and telecommunications networks necessary to make electronic money the __8__ form of payment. Second, paper checks have the advantage that they __9__ receipts, something that many consumers are unwilling to __10__. Third, the use of paper checks gives consumers several days of “float” – it takes several days __11__ a check is cashed and funds are __12__ from the issuer’s account, which means that the writer of the check can earn interest on the funds in the meantime. __13__ electronic payments arc immediate, they eliminate the float for the consumer.

Fourth, electronic means of payment may __14__ security and privacy concerns. We often hear media reports that an unauthorized hacker has been able to access a computer database and to alter information __15__ there. The fact that this is not an __16__ occurrence means that dishonest persons might be able to access bank accounts in electronic payments systems and __17__ from someone else’s accounts. The __18__ of this type of fraud is no easy task, and a new field of computer science is developing to __19__ security issues. A further concern is that the use of electronic means of payment leaves an electronic __20__ that contains a large amount of personal data. 答案

答案：A

10

本题讲解：2013 完形第 10 空 · 消费者不愿对收据做什么——give up

答案：A（give up 放弃；舍弃）。

第 0 步：先立机制——链式空：上一空定下一空

空格在 something that many consumers are unwilling to ___ 的定语从句谓语位，something 回指 receipts。方向由上一环锁死：第 9 空确立“纸质支票提供收据”是 advantage——好处才有人不肯撒手。所以空格要表达“放弃、舍弃”，与 unwilling 组合成“不愿放弃收据”，反向支撑纸票的优势论。

第 1 步：定位原文

Second, paper checks have the advantage that they provide receipts, something that many consumers are unwilling to __10__.

unwilling to ___ receipts：动词短语位。give up / take over / bring back / pass down 四个短语义域各异，先由 advantage 定方向“舍弃”，再核对各短语的使用场景。

第 2 步：找证据——advantage → 收据是想要的东西 → 不愿舍弃

provide receipts（提供收据）与 unwilling to give up（不愿放弃）一给一留，逻辑闭环。这正是出题人用两空联动设置的“链式题”：任何一空脱离另一空单独选，四个选项里至少有两个能“读通”，必须靠链条互相锁定。

第 3 步：逐项排除

选项词义判定理由与典型误解
A give up放弃；舍弃✓ 当选unwilling to give up receipts = 不愿舍弃收据——收据有用才显出纸票优势，与第 9 空 provide 闭环。（易错警示：bring back 字面顺手；收据本就在手，无“回”可言。）
B take over接管；接手✗ 排除take over 的宾语一般是业务、职位、公司，“接管收据”讲不通。（典型误解：把“取得（收据）”的直觉错配成 take over。）
C bring back带回；使恢复✗ 排除收据本来就在消费者手里，谈不上“带回来”。（典型误解：见 bring 就配 back。）
D pass down遗传；传下✗ 排除pass down 指（代际）传承，宾语多为传统、手艺，收据没有“传下去”的语境。（典型误解：短语义域不清，凭字面猜。）

代入后的完整句义：其次，纸质支票有一个优势：它们能提供收据——许多消费者不愿舍弃的东西。

第 4 步：预判追问

  • give up 还有哪些高频义？：放弃（尝试）、戒除（习惯）、交出让出（give up one’s seat）；本题为“舍弃”义：unwilling to give up receipts = 不愿舍弃收据。
  • 为什么必须先固定第 9 空？：advantage → 收据是消费者想要的东西 → 不愿“舍弃”。若把上一环读成劣势，四个短语的排序全部重洗——链式空必须先固上一环再动下一环。

易错点

  • 误选 B（take over）：把“取得（收据）”的直觉错配成 take over。
  • 误选 C（bring back）：见 bring 就配 back。
  • 误选 D（pass down）：短语义域不清，凭字面猜。
记忆锚点：unwilling to give up = 不愿放弃；链式空：上一空的词义方向是下一空的锚；pass down 只用于“传承”。
