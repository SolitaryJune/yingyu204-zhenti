48. Directions:
Write an essay based on the following table. In your writing, you should
1) describe the table, and
2) give your comments.
You should write at least 150 words.
Write your essay on ANSWER SHEET 2. (15 points)
某公司员工工作满意度调查
满意度
满意 不清楚 不满意
年龄组
≤40 岁 16.7％ 50.0％ 33.3％
41~50 岁 0.0％ 36.0％ 64.0％
＞50 岁 40.0％ 50.0％ 10.0％

范文

Employees above 50 years old were most likely to report satisfaction, with 40% of them indicating they were content. Employees 40 years or younger were less willing to declare satisfaction, only 16.7% of whom said they were satisfied; instead, a third of whom expressed dissatisfaction. Employees in the 41-to-50 age group were surely unhappiest: none of them felt satisfied and the percentage of respondents reporting dissatisfaction was as high as 64%.
There is a significant difference in job satisfaction by employee age. The difference relates to their career situation. Employees at the age from 41 to 50, less energetic than younger crowd and less competent than older professionals, reach a career plateau. The consequent sense of powerlessness is strong enough to reduce them to deep dissatisfaction. In contrast, younger colleagues, in spite of occasional confusion, are mostly in an upward spiral and understandably will feel happier. As for elder colleagues, many of them have achieved career maturity and their careers are going to be crowned with relaxing retirement life. Not surprisingly, they are the happiest.
It is clear that career troubles will afflict people at all ages. For lasting happiness with our jobs, positive attitude is necessary.

自评得分：
48

本题讲解：2012 大作文——满意度「U 型」表格的极值描图 → 处境析因 → 态度收束

答案（参考范文口径）：Employees above 50 years old were most likely to report satisfaction, with 40% of them indicating they were content. Employees 40 years or younger were less willing to declare satisfaction, only 16.7% of whom said they were satisfied; instead, a third of whom expressed dissatisfaction. Employees in the 41-to-50 age group were surely unhappiest: none of them felt satisfied and the percentage of respondents reporting dissatisfaction was as high as 64%. There is a significant difference in job satisfaction by employee age. The difference relates to their career situation. Employees at the age from 41 to 50, less energetic than younger crowd and less competent than older professionals, reach a career plateau. The consequent sense of powerlessness is strong enough to reduce them to deep dissatisfaction. In contrast, younger colleagues, in spite of occasional confusion, are mostly in an upward spiral and understandably will feel happier. As for elder colleagues, many of them have achieved career maturity and their careers are going to be crowned with relaxing retirement life. Not surprisingly, they are the happiest. It is clear that career troubles will afflict people at all ages. For lasting happiness with our jobs, positive attitude is necessary.

第 0 步：先立机制——表格作文的分数从哪来

两问换三段：describe the table（描图段）+ give your comments（析因段 + 评论段）。表格作文的机制三条：表格不逐格念，抓"极值 + 中间值"；析因不凭空想，从数据反推人群处境；评论不另起炉灶，从原因里收束。只描图不评论 = 只答第一问，结构掉档；析因与数据脱节 = 臆测扣分。机制立住，三段就是往里填内容。

第 1 步：读图——先定对比轴：满意度随年龄呈"U 型"

年龄组满意不清楚不满意
≤40 岁16.7%50.0%33.3%
41~50 岁0.0%36.0%64.0%
＞50 岁40.0%50.0%10.0%
  • 极值：满意比例最高 = 50 岁以上（40%，不满意仅 10%）；满意比例最低 = 41~50 岁（0%，不满意高达 64%）。
  • 中间值：40 岁及以下（满意 16.7%，不满意 33.3%）。
  • 对比轴不是"谁高谁低"的罗列，而是满意度随年龄呈 U 型：中间年龄段垫底。这块"中年洼地"就是全文要解释的矛盾——描图段写出落差，析因段解释洼地成因，评论段收束。

第 2 步：描图段——极值 → 中间 → 最低，序中带评

  • 描图不逐格念：三句话覆盖三个年龄组，每组只报"满意 + 不满意"两个关键数字，"不清楚"一列可略。
  • 顺序即态度：先报最高（>50 岁 40% 满意）→ 再报中间（≤40 岁仅 16.7% 满意、三分之一不满）→ 压轴报最低（41~50 岁 0% 满意、64% 不满）。递降到最低组收尾，反差最强。
  • 数据动词轮换不重复：report satisfaction / declare satisfaction / express dissatisfaction 三个"表态"动词各用一次。

第 3 步：句型升格——数据写进从句，不写成流水账

  • with 独立主格伴随补比例：with 40% of them indicating they were content。
  • whom 非限制性定语从句把两笔数据焊进一句：only 16.7% of whom said they were satisfied; instead, a third of whom expressed dissatisfaction——instead 转折点出"不满多于满意"。
  • 冒号引并列分句压轴：...were surely unhappiest: none of them felt satisfied and the percentage of respondents reporting dissatisfaction was as high as 64%——冒号相当于连词引出解释，as high as 强调不满之众，reporting dissatisfaction 分词后置定语压缩从句。

第 4 步：析因段——从数据反推人群处境

  • 主题句先立框架：There is a significant difference in job satisfaction by employee age. The difference relates to their career situation.——"差异存在 + 与职场处境相关"，归因才有落点。
  • 中年洼地（41~50 岁）的机制是"上下夹击"，三级因果链：比年轻人体力弱（less energetic than younger crowd）、比年长者资历浅（less competent than older professionals）→ reach a career plateau（进入职场高原，停滞不前）→ consequent sense of powerlessness（无力感）→ deep dissatisfaction。0% 与 64% 两个数据被这条链解释掉了。
  • U 型两端反向闭合：年轻人 occasional confusion 但 in an upward spiral（整体在上升期）→ feel happier；年长者 career maturity（职业发展成熟）+ 退休生活可期（crowned with relaxing retirement life）→ happiest。

第 5 步：评论段——从析因收束，不喊口号

  • It is clear that career troubles will afflict people at all ages.：把"洼地在中间"推普成"职场困扰人人有份"。
  • For lasting happiness with our jobs, positive attitude is necessary.：建议落在"态度"上——因为析因显示落差源于处境与预期，态度是个人可控变量。评论两句话，都长在析因上，不是另起炉灶的口号。

易错点：每个失分点对应哪种误解

  • 逐格罗列 9 个数据：描图变流水账，主次全无——表格只抓极值与中间值。
  • 只描不析：第二问 give your comments 缺失，结构掉档。
  • 析因凭空：写"因为工资低/老板苛刻"等表格之外的因素——析因必须从人群处境反推（体力、资历、晋升空间），与数据自洽。
  • 时态错：调查已发生，描图用过去时（were / said / expressed / felt），全篇现在时是时态纪律失分。
  • 数据表达单一：全篇 only said，没有 whom 从句与 as high as 的强调层次，句式扁平。
  • 评论与析因脱节：空喊 work hard 而不接"处境差异 → 态度可控"的逻辑链。
记忆锚点：表格作文 = 极值描图（U 型对比轴）→ 处境析因（上下夹击 → 职场高原 → 无力感）→ 态度收束；句型靠 whom 从句 + 冒号分句 + as high as；数据动词 report / declare / express 轮换；描图过去时。
