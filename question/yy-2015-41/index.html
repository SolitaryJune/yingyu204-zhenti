Part B
Directions:
Read the following text and answer the questions by choosing the most suitable
subheading from the list A-G for each of the numbered paragraphs (41-45). There are
two extra subheadings which you do not need to use. Mark your answers on the
ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

Some Old Truths to Help You Overcome Tough Times

Unfortunately, life is not a bed of roses. We are going through life facing sad experiences. Moreover, we are grieving various kinds of loss: a friendship, a romantic relationship or a house. Hard times may hold you down at what usually seems like the most inopportune time, but you should remember that they won’t last forever.

When our time of mourning is over, we press forward, stronger with a greater understanding and respect for life. Furthermore, these losses make us mature and eventually move us toward future opportunities for growth and happiness. I want to share these old truths I’ve learned along the way.

【41】 Fear is both useful and harmful. This normal human reaction is used to protect us by signaling danger and preparing us to deal with it. Unfortunately, people create inner barriers with a help of exaggerating fears. My favorite actor Will Smith once said, “Fear is not real. It is a product of thoughts you create. Do not misunderstand me. Danger is very real. But fear is a choice.” I do completely agree that fears are just the product of our luxuriant imagination.

【42】 If you are surrounded by problems and cannot stop thinking about the past, try to focus on the present moment. Many of us are weighed down by the past or anxious about the future. You may feel guilt over your past, but you are poisoning the present with the things and circumstances you cannot change. Value the present moment and remember how fortunate you are to be alive. Enjoy the beauty of the world around and keep the eyes open to see the possibilities before you. Happiness is not a point of future and not a moment from the past, but a mindset that can be designed into the present.

【43】 Sometimes it is easy to feel bad because you are going through tough times. You can be easily caught up by life problems that you forget to pause and appreciate the things you have. Only strong people prefer to smile and value their life instead of crying and complaining about something.

【44】 No matter how isolated you might feel and how serious the situation is, you should always remember that you are not alone. Try to keep in mind that almost everyone respects and wants to help you if you are trying to make a good change in your life, especially your dearest and nearest people. You may have a circle of friends who provide constant good humor, help and companionship. If you have no friends or relatives, try to participate in several online communities, full of people who are always willing to share advice and encouragement.

【45】 Today many people find it difficult to trust their own opinion and seek balance by gaining objectivity from external sources. This way you devalue your opinion and show that you are incapable of managing your own life. When you are struggling to achieve something important you should believe in yourself and be sure that your decision is the best. You live in your skin, think your own thoughts, have your own values and make your own choices.

答案

答案：D

41

本题讲解：小标题题【41】——"恐惧"段的中心落在转折之后

答案：D（Most of your fears are unreal）。

第 0 步：先立方法——小标题 = 段落主旨的一句话压缩

给段落选小标题，动作只有两个：找讨论对象（本段反复围绕的名词）和找作者对它的判断（尤其是转折词之后的判断）。七选五式小标题还有一条暗规则：两个用不上的"多余项"往往是盯住各段字面词汇做的诱饵——谁的词眼熟就冒充谁的标题。

第 1 步：定位段落，抓转折结构

Fear is both useful and harmful. ... Unfortunately, people create inner barriers with a help of exaggerating fears. ... "Fear is not real. It is a product of thoughts you create. ... Danger is very real. But fear is a choice." I do completely agree that fears are just the product of our luxuriant imagination.

结构一目了然：①首句让步式承认"恐惧有用也有害"；②Unfortunately 转折——人们夸大恐惧，给自己筑起内心的屏障；③引威尔·史密斯名言：Fear is not real，恐惧是你自己念头造出来的产物，危险真实，恐惧是一种选择；④尾句作者表态：恐惧只是丰富想象力的产物。重心全在转折之后。

第 2 步：对号入座

讨论对象 = fears；作者判断 = not real / a product of thoughts you create / a product of our luxuriant imagination。[D] Most of your fears are unreal：fears 复现对象，unreal 一词把上面三处表述一并压缩。

第 3 步：排除诱饵（干扰项归因）

选项判定归因
F Don't fear responsibility for your life✗字面诱饵：抓住段中 fear 一词，但段落谈的是"恐惧多半不是真的"，通篇没有 responsibility（人生责任）的话题
B Experience helps you grow✗错位嫁接："经历助成长"是文章引言的教训（losses make us mature ... growth and happiness），不是【41】段的中心

其余 A、C、E、G 各有归宿（分别对应【44】【45】【42】【43】段），本段内容与之无涉。

预判追问：首句"恐惧有用"为什么不进标题？

both useful and harmful 是让步铺垫，Unfortunately 转折之后才是作者立论；标题概括的是重心句而非让步句。这与阅读细节题"but 之后是重点"是同一套机制——转折结构不分题型地起作用。

记忆锚点：小标题 = 对象 + 转折后的判断；Fear is not real / product of imagination = unreal；fear 字面复现 ≠ F 的 responsibility。
