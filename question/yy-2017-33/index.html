Read the following four texts. Answer the questions after each text by choosing A, B, C or D. Mark your answers on the ANSWER SHEET. (40 points)

Today, widespread social pressure to immediately go to college in conjunction with increasingly high expectations in a fast-moving world often causes students to completely overlook the possibility of taking a gap year. After all, if everyone you know is going to college in the fall, it seems silly to stay back a year, doesn’t it? And after going to school for 12 years, it doesn’t feel natural to spend a year doing something that isn’t academic.

But while this may be true, it’s not a good enough reason to condemn gap years. There’s always a constant fear of falling behind everyone else on the socially perpetuated “race to the finish line,” whether that be toward graduate school, medical school or a lucrative career. But despite common misconceptions, a gap year does not hinder the success of academic pursuits – in fact, it probably enhances it.

Studies from the United States and Australia show that students who take a gap year are generally better prepared for and perform better in college than those who do not. Rather than pulling students back, a gap year pushes them ahead by preparing them for independence, new responsibilities and environmental changes – all things that first-year students often struggle with the most. Gap year experiences can lessen the blow when it comes to adjusting to college and being thrown into a brand new environment, making it easier to focus on academics and activities rather than acclimation blunders.

If you’re not convinced of the inherent value in taking a year off to explore interests, then consider its financial impact on future academic choices. According to the National Center for Education Statistics, nearly 80 percent of college students end up changing their majors at least once. This isn’t surprising, considering the basic mandatory high school curriculum leaves students with a poor understanding of the vast academic possibilities that await them in college. Many students find themselves listing one major on their college applications, but switching to another after taking college classes. It’s not necessarily a bad thing, but depending on the school, it can be costly to make up credits after switching too late in the game. At Boston College, for example, you would have to complete an extra year were you to switch to the nursing school from another department. Taking a gap year to figure things out initially can help prevent stress and save money later on.

The word “acclimation” (Para. 3) is closest in meaning to .

本题讲解：2017 Text C 第 33 题 · acclimation 词义推断

答案：A（adaptation 适应）。

第 0 步：先立机制——词义题的词义从上下文来，不从词典来

acclimation 是超纲词，命题人根本不指望你认识它。词义推断题的机制：词义藏在上下文的三样东西里——逻辑关系词（rather than / but / while）、指代回指、前后语义的复现或对立。本句骨架是 focus on A rather than B：专注 A、摆脱 B——B 必须与 A 对立，还得是间隔年能“减轻”的东西。

第 1 步：定位原文

Gap year experiences can lessen the blow when it comes to adjusting to college and being thrown into a brand new environment, making it easier to focus on academics and activities rather than acclimation blunders.（第三段③句）

第 2 步：找证据——上一句已把“新生难事”摆好

Rather than pulling students back, a gap year pushes them ahead by preparing them for independence, new responsibilities and environmental changes – all things that first-year students often struggle with the most.（第三段②句）

②句交代了新生最 struggle 的三件事，其中就有环境变化；③句 adjusting to college / brand new environment 原样复现这一话题，lessen the blow（减轻冲击）减的正是“适应之难”。被 rather than 甩到对立面的 acclimation blunders，只能是适应期出的岔子——若不存在适应问题，它就拖不住你专注学业与活动。

第 3 步：逐项排除

选项词义判定理由与典型误解
A adaptation适应✓ 当选adjusting to college and being thrown into a brand new environment 的概括复现；与“专注学业与活动”构成取舍的两端。
B application申请✗ 排除影子来自第四段 college applications（专业申请），跨段挪词；本段话题是“适应新环境”。（典型误解：见词形相近就配对，不查出处段落。）
C motivation积极性✗ 排除从②句 pushes them ahead 臆测出“干劲”；但 rather than 之后是被摆脱的绊脚石，不是动力，“专注学业而非积极性”读不通。（典型误解：没解出 rather than 的取舍方向。）
D competition竞争✗ 排除出处是第二段“race to the finish line”的落后焦虑；“专注学业而非竞争问题”与“适应大学”话题无关。（典型误解：不就近取线索，跑到两段之外找词义。）

代入后的完整句义：间隔年经历能减轻（适应大学的）冲击，让学生更容易专注于学业与活动，而不被适应期的失误拖累。

第 4 步：预判追问

  • acclimation blunders 整体是什么？：blunder 也是超纲词（大错、失误）。两个超纲词连用，当作一个整体“适应期的失误”理解即可，不必拆开硬猜。
  • 为什么说 A 是“概括复现”？：adjusting to college 写的是动作过程，adaptation 是其名词化概括。词义题正确项的常见形态，正是用考纲内熟词概括原文表述——“考点用生词、答案用熟词”是命题习惯。

易错点

  • 误选 B（application）：被第四段 college applications 带跑——跨段挪词是词义题第一陷阱。
  • 误选 C（motivation）：取舍方向装反，把“绊脚石”位读成“动力”位。
记忆锚点：词义题三线索——逻辑词（rather than：前要后舍）、回指、同义复现（adjusting to a new environment ↔ adaptation）；acclimation blunders = 适应期失误。
