本题讲解：2017 新题型第 42 题 · Jason Stenquist 的观点

答案：A（says that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.）。

第 0 步：先立机制——观点本体在 says + 引号处，场景描写不算观点

（三步法同第 41 题：人名定位 → 观点标记词 → 同义替换。）本题的额外提醒：人物段落常先给场景描写（Stenquist 上手第一周、见访客发怵），那不是观点；观点本体永远落在 says + 双引号处。

第 1 步：定位原文

Asked about his choice of career, he says at high school he considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering. “I love working with tools. I love creating,” he says.（第七段④⑤句）

第 2 步：找证据——职业选择 + 理由

says 管两处：转述他“由医学转向电气工程”，直引他“爱摆弄工具、爱创造”。与 A 项比对：switched to electrical engineering、loves working with tools 几乎原词复现，because 恰好承载“理由”关系。

第 3 步：逐项排除（归位到主人）

选项观点内容判定主人与依据
Asays that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.✓ 当选（本题）Jason Stenquist（第七段）：considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering；“I love working with tools. I love creating.”
Bpoints out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.✗ 排除Rob Spohr（第十段）：“There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill.”
Cpoints out that the US doesn’t manufacture anything anymore.✗ 排除Donald Trump（第一段）：“We don't make anything anymore,” he told Fox News
Dbelieves that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.✗ 排除Robert Roth（第六段）：keeps a close eye on the age of his nearly 200 workers
Esays that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.✗ 排除Jay Dunwell（第五段）：“They're harder to find and they have job offers” + For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers
Fpoints out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.✗ 排除Julie Parks（第十一段）：points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance
Gsays that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people’s parents.✗ 排除Birgit Klohs（第八段）：“remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession”

第 4 步：预判追问

  • D（关注员工年龄）为什么有人误选？：同是“工厂里的年轻人”语境；但 keep a close eye on the age 是第六段 Roth（管理者）的动作，与 Stenquist（一线新工人）身份不合——人物身份也是比对的辅助信号。
  • flustered（发怵）是他的观点吗？：不是，是作者对他的神态描写，没有观点价值。

易错点

  • 误选 D（believes that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.）：被“工厂”语境带跑，忘查主人。
  • 把场景描写当观点：前三句是画面，④⑤句才是话。
记忆锚点：场景描写 ≠ 观点；says + 双引号才是观点本体；人物身份（一线工人 vs 老板/教授）辅助排除。
