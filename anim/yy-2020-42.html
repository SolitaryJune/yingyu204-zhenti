本题讲解：新题型小标题——等比句式把重心压给"倾听他人"

答案：F（Slow down and listen）。

第一步：先立机制——Just as important as A is B，重心在 B

段首 Just as important as being honest about yourself is being receptive to others 是倒装等比句：A 与 B 同等重要，重心落在后面的 B——本段主线是"接纳/倾听他人"，而非"坦诚自己"（那是 41 段的主线）。先定重心，再看细节。

第二步：定位原文——建议句

Those are all valid, but you need to take time to hear out your coworkers, too. In fact, rushing to get your own ideas out there can cause colleagues to feel you don't value their opinions. Do your best to engage coworkers in a genuine, back-and-forth conversation, rather than prioritizing your own thoughts.

表达想法没错，但要 take time to hear out coworkers（听同事说完）；危害警示：rushing（急于输出）→ 同事觉得你不重视其意见；建议：真诚的一来一回对话，别让自己的想法优先。

第三步：对号入座与排除

  • F Slow down and listen ✓：slow down ↔ take time / not rushing / rather than prioritizing your own thoughts；listen ↔ receptive / hear out / back-and-forth。
  • A Give compliments, just not too many ✗：compliment 原词复现，但它只是段中列举"我们想说的话"之一（a concern, a stray thought, or a compliment），本段没给"赞美的度"任何建议。
  • E Reveal, don't hide, information ✗：与 being honest 近义，但等比句式已把重心压给"倾听他人"——E 是 41 段的主线，偏离本段重心。

易错点

  • 误选 A（最强干扰）：原词复现陷阱——例举项 ≠ 段旨。
  • 误选 E：见 honest 相关词就选，不辨句式重心。
记忆锚点：Just as important as A is B → 重心在 B；hear out / back-and-forth = listen；原词复现 ≠ 正确。
