本题讲解：2017 新题型第 45 题 · Julie Parks 的观点

答案：F（points out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.）。

第 0 步：先立机制——观点标记词不止 says

（三步法同前。）本题看点：标记词是 points to（指出）——观点标记词家族：says / points to / notes / argues / 双引号。见到人名先圈标记词，标记词辖到哪，观点就到哪。

第 1 步：定位原文

Julie Parks of Grand Rapids Community College points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance. While their parents were content to work long hours, young people value flexibility. “Overtime is not attractive to this generation. They really want to live their lives,” she says.（第十一段①②句）

第 2 步：找证据——冒号后的关键词

points to 点出的“关键”是 a work/life balance；后续两句补充理由：老一辈甘愿加班，年轻人看重弹性、要过自己的生活。与 F 项比对：a work/life balance 原词照搬；attract ↔ luring；young people ↔ Millennials。

第 3 步：逐项排除（归位到主人）

选项观点内容判定主人与依据
Asays that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.✗ 排除Jason Stenquist（第七段）：considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering；“I love working with tools. I love creating.”
Bpoints out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.✗ 排除Rob Spohr（第十段）：“There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill.”
Cpoints out that the US doesn’t manufacture anything anymore.✗ 排除Donald Trump（第一段）：“We don't make anything anymore,” he told Fox News
Dbelieves that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.✗ 排除Robert Roth（第六段）：keeps a close eye on the age of his nearly 200 workers
Esays that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.✗ 排除Jay Dunwell（第五段）：“They're harder to find and they have job offers” + For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers
Fpoints out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.✓ 当选（本题）Julie Parks（第十一段）：points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance
Gsays that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people’s parents.✗ 排除Birgit Klohs（第八段）：“remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession”

第 4 步：预判追问

  • luring 为什么算替换 attract？：lure = 吸引、诱使，attract = 吸引，且后面都接 into manufacturing——同义词对。
  • 本篇七选项为何每题都全列？：命题把全篇观点都放进右列，多余的用来干扰——所以每题只做一件事：本题人物的观点 vs 选项的主人。

易错点

  • 漏掉 points to：只认识 says，跟丢标记词。
  • 误选 B（points out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.）：同话题不同主人——“低技能岗位不缺人”出自第十段 Spohr。
记忆锚点：观点标记词家族——says / points to / notes / argues / 双引号；lure ↔ attract；冒号后的名词短语常是观点关键词。
