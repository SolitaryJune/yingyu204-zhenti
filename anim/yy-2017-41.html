本题讲解：2017 新题型第 41 题 · Jay Dunwell 的观点

答案：E（says that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.）。

第 0 步：先立机制——人物—观点匹配三步法

本篇是“人物—观点”匹配：左列 5 个人物，右列 7 个观点（2 个多余）。解法三步：①人名回文定位——人名只出现在某一段，直接跳读；②抓观点标记词——says / points to / 双引号，标记词管到哪，观点就到哪；③同义替换比对——选项几乎都是原文观点的改写。全篇 7 个观点恰好分属 7 个人物（Trump、Roth、Stenquist、Dunwell、Klohs、Spohr、Parks），一人一项、无共用——定位即破题。

第 1 步：定位原文

For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers – and upward pressure on wages. “They're harder to find and they have job offers,” says Jay Dunwell... “they've been plucked by other industries that are also doing as well as manufacturing.”（第五段①—③句）

第 2 步：找证据——观点标记词 + 关键词比对

①句给出观点的土壤（对工厂主而言 = 工人争夺战白热化）；②③句 says + 双引号亮出观点本体：工人更难找、手上揣着别家 offer、被其他行业抢走。与 E 项比对：for factory owners、harder to find、stiff competition 三处对应；plucked by other industries（被别家抢走）正是“竞争激烈”的具体化。

第 3 步：逐项排除（归位到主人）

选项观点内容判定主人与依据
Asays that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.✗ 排除Jason Stenquist（第七段）：considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering；“I love working with tools. I love creating.”
Bpoints out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.✗ 排除Rob Spohr（第十段）：“There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill.”
Cpoints out that the US doesn’t manufacture anything anymore.✗ 排除Donald Trump（第一段）：“We don't make anything anymore,” he told Fox News
Dbelieves that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.✗ 排除Robert Roth（第六段）：keeps a close eye on the age of his nearly 200 workers
Esays that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.✓ 当选（本题）Jay Dunwell（第五段）：“They're harder to find and they have job offers” + For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers
Fpoints out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.✗ 排除Julie Parks（第十一段）：points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance
Gsays that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people’s parents.✗ 排除Birgit Klohs（第八段）：“remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession”

第 4 步：预判追问

  • stiff competition 算“同义替换”吗？：①句 it all adds up to stiff competition for workers 是 Dunwell 观点的导语，②句 harder to find 是它的展开；E 把“土壤 + 本体”合写成一句，三关键词复现，属高保真改写。
  • 多余选项从哪来？：题目 5 人物之外，Trump、Roth 的观点也被编进右列充数——“逐项查户口”是排除的钥匙：每个观点都找主人，不属于题干人物的全部划走。

易错点

  • 误选 B（points out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.）：同在“用工”话题下，但“低技能岗位人力充足”是第十段 Rob Spohr 的角度。
  • 只盯引号、漏掉①句：harder to find 单看也能选对，但少了 stiff competition 的复核链——定位、比对要整套做。
记忆锚点：匹配题三步——人名定位 → says/points to/引号 → 同义替换比对；7 个观点一人一项，逐项查户口。
