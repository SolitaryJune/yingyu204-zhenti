本题讲解：2017 新题型第 43 题 · Birgit Klohs 的观点

答案：G（says that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people’s parents.）。

第 0 步：先立机制——观点标记词可以“尾挂”

（三步法同前。）本题看点：says Birgit Klohs 挂在句末（尾挂式）——引号内容是她的转述，标记词管住前面整句。认准引号辖域即可，不必纠结标记词位置。

第 1 步：定位原文

Millennials “remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession,” says Birgit Klohs, chief executive of The Right Place...（第八段②句）

第 2 步：找证据——两处同义替换

与 G 项比对：the lay-off of the young people's parents ↔ father and mother both were laid off；is to blame for ↔ They blame it on。语义完全等值，高保真改写。

第 3 步：逐项排除（归位到主人）

选项观点内容判定主人与依据
Asays that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.✗ 排除Jason Stenquist（第七段）：considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering；“I love working with tools. I love creating.”
Bpoints out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.✗ 排除Rob Spohr（第十段）：“There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill.”
Cpoints out that the US doesn’t manufacture anything anymore.✗ 排除Donald Trump（第一段）：“We don't make anything anymore,” he told Fox News
Dbelieves that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.✗ 排除Robert Roth（第六段）：keeps a close eye on the age of his nearly 200 workers
Esays that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.✗ 排除Jay Dunwell（第五段）：“They're harder to find and they have job offers” + For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers
Fpoints out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.✗ 排除Julie Parks（第十一段）：points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance
Gsays that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people’s parents.✓ 当选（本题）Birgit Klohs（第八段）：“remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession”

第 4 步：预判追问

  • C 也谈“制造业衰退”，为什么不是 Klohs？：话题相近但主人是第一段 Trump，且 C 是 Trump 的原话“We don't make anything anymore”——夸张的竞选式断言，与 Klohs 转述的“父母被裁、归咎衰退”内容不同。
  • laid off 与 lay-off 什么关系？：前者动词（被解雇），后者名词化（解雇事件）——动名转换是同义替换的常见手法。

易错点

  • 误选 C（points out that the US doesn’t manufacture anything anymore.）：见“制造业衰退”就配，不查主人。
  • 尾挂式标记词跟丢：says 在句末，只顾句首找人名的考生会漏。
记忆锚点：观点标记尾挂（..., says X）同样有效，管住前面的引号；blame it on ↔ is to blame for、laid off ↔ lay-off。
