本题讲解：2017 新题型第 44 题 · Rob Spohr 的观点

答案：B（points out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.）。

第 0 步：先立机制——选项可以只改写观点的“某半句”

（三步法同前。）本题看点：Spohr 的观点有两层——“缺口卡在中间地带” + “低技能岗位不缺人”；B 只改写了后半句。匹配题的选项不要求穷尽人物观点，与观点一致即可。

第 1 步：定位原文

“The gap is between the jobs that take no skills and those that require a lot of skill,” says Rob Spohr, a business professor... “There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill. It's that gap in between, and that's where the problem is.”（第十段①②句）

第 2 步：找证据——原词复现 + 从句压缩

与 B 项比对：there are enough people to fill 原词照搬；where you don't need to have much skill（定语从句）压缩为 that don't need much skill。B 是 Spohr 引语后半句的忠实改写。

第 3 步：逐项排除（归位到主人）

选项观点内容判定主人与依据
Asays that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.✗ 排除Jason Stenquist（第七段）：considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering；“I love working with tools. I love creating.”
Bpoints out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don’t need much skill.✓ 当选（本题）Rob Spohr（第十段）：“There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill.”
Cpoints out that the US doesn’t manufacture anything anymore.✗ 排除Donald Trump（第一段）：“We don't make anything anymore,” he told Fox News
Dbelieves that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.✗ 排除Robert Roth（第六段）：keeps a close eye on the age of his nearly 200 workers
Esays that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.✗ 排除Jay Dunwell（第五段）：“They're harder to find and they have job offers” + For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers
Fpoints out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.✗ 排除Julie Parks（第十一段）：points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance
Gsays that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people’s parents.✗ 排除Birgit Klohs（第八段）：“remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession”

第 4 步：预判追问

  • E 为什么诱惑大？：同属“用工”话题。但 Dunwell 说的是“制造业抢不到人”（竞争角度），Spohr 说的是“结构性缺口”（技能分层角度）——E 的主人是第五段 Dunwell。
  • B 与 Spohr 的主旨（gap in between）矛盾吗？：不矛盾。B 引的是他话里“低技能岗位不缺人”半句，恰是他论证“缺口在中间”的对比面——部分复现依然与观点一致。

易错点

  • 误选 E（says that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.）：话题相近，不查主人。
  • 要求选项穷尽观点：B 未提“缺口在中间”就犹豫——匹配题只要一致。
记忆锚点：选项可只改写某半句——一致即可，不必穷尽；enough people to fill 是原词复现信号。
