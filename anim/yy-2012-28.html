本题讲解：长句拆主宾——dictate 的主语才是影响源

答案：B（the marketing of products for children）。

第一步：先立机制——因果细节题先分清"谁影响谁"

题干 our perception of children's psychological development was much influenced by ___ 要求找影响源。英语长句里这类动词（dictate / influence / shape / determine）的主语才是施加影响的一方。

第二步：定位原文——第三段①句

I had not realised how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development.

影响源 = marketing trends（dictate"支配"的主语）；被影响对象有两个——perception of what is natural to kids 与 beliefs about psychological development，后者正是题干所问。

第三步：逐项排除

  • B the marketing of products for children ✓：influenced ↔ dictated；marketing of products for children ↔ marketing trends。④句例证：toddler 阶段就是服装制造商 20 世纪 30 年代用营销手段推广的。
  • A the observation of children's nature ✗：对儿童天性的看法与题干所问是并列的两个"被影响对象"——把难兄难弟当成元凶。
  • C researches into children's behaviour ✗：③句 I assumed that phase was something experts developed after years of research...: wrong——前说后否，作者恰好否定"专家研究说"。
  • D studies of childhood consumption ✗：childhood consumerism 只是 Cook 的头衔定语（a historian of childhood consumerism），不构成影响源。

易错点

  • 误选 A（最强干扰）：including 引出的并列成分与题干关键词同处一句，极易误当因果；拆清主宾即可避开。
  • 误选 C：漏读冒号后的 wrong，把"作者已否定的旧观点"当成答案。
记忆锚点：dictate / influence 的主语 = 影响源；assumed...: wrong = 前说后否。
